Andromeda Antivirus - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Andromeda Antivirus ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

Andromeda Antivirus collects the following information to provide security scanning and threat detection:

  • Scan Logs: File names, file paths, and detection results from security scans you initiate
  • Device Information: Android OS version, device model, and app performance metrics
  • Usage Statistics: Frequency of scans, features used, and app crashes (for improvement purposes)
  • Threat Data: Malware signatures and threat definitions detected on your device

We do NOT collect:

  • Personal identification information (names, emails, phone numbers) unless you contact support
  • App data or user files (only metadata related to security threats)
  • Browsing history or location data
  • Financial or payment information

3. Permissions Explained

Andromeda Antivirus requests the following permissions to function:

  • Device Admin: Required to scan system files and detect threats effectively
  • Storage Access: Needed to scan files and apps on your device
  • Internet: To download threat definitions and send usage statistics (optional, can be disabled)
  • Package Usage Stats: To monitor app behavior and detect suspicious activity

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To perform security scans and detect malware or threats
  • To improve app performance and add new security features
  • To send security alerts when threats are detected
  • To generate anonymized threat reports and statistics

We will never:

  • Sell or share your personal data with third parties
  • Use your data for marketing or advertising purposes
  • Share threat data with unauthorized services

5. Data Retention

Scan logs and usage statistics are retained on your device only. We do not store your data on remote servers. Threat detection data is updated automatically as new threats emerge and old definitions are replaced.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Scan results are encrypted on your device and never transmitted without your consent.

7. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access what data the App has collected (via app settings)
  • Delete all scan logs and usage data from your device at any time
  • Disable threat reporting and cloud synchronization features
  • Uninstall the App and remove all associated data

8. Third-Party Services

Andromeda Antivirus does not integrate with third-party analytics or tracking services. All processing is done locally on your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "Last updated" date above indicates when this policy was last changed. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of any changes.
